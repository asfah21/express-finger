# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AZRA is used by employees at a shared attendance kiosk and by superadmins in an administrative dashboard. Employees need a fast, low-friction way to clock in or out using face recognition. Superadmins manage employees, devices, attendance records, and the Live recognition workflow.

## Product Purpose

AZRA connects fingerprint-device attendance operations with a web dashboard and a camera-based Live kiosk. Success means an employee can choose Masuk or Pulang, present their face, and receive a clear attendance result without needing an account, while administrators retain trustworthy records and operational control.

## Brand Personality

Fast, clear, trustworthy. The interface should feel calm and dependable during a busy attendance window, and approachable enough for people who do not use the administrative dashboard every day.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative or complicated kiosk UI, dense admin-only terminology in employee-facing flows, ambiguous status messages, hidden camera permissions, and visual effects that slow down or distract from attendance capture.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious: the kiosk should lead with Masuk and Pulang.
- Show system state honestly: camera, recognition, success, duplicate, and failure states must be explicit.
- Keep attendance trustworthy: identify the employee before recording and preserve the existing attendance semantics.
- Optimize for shared-device use: large controls, readable feedback, automatic reset, and no login requirement.
- Keep administration powerful without leaking administrative complexity into the kiosk.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for the new interface. Use strong text contrast, large touch targets, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, status text that does not rely on color alone, reduced-motion support, and clear fallback messages when camera permissions or face recognition are unavailable.
